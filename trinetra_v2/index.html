03 / GROUNDED HARDWARE BOM

Hardware Architecture & Sensor Selection

Every sensor and processor is selected from the uploaded SIH 26026 engineering research for low-power edge computing, high sensitivity, and rugged railway field reliability.

Edge Processing Computer

Raspberry Pi 5

Central edge computer featuring a 2.4 GHz quad-core 64-bit Arm Cortex-A76 processor. Executes sensor fusion and on-device neural network inference in under 42ms while operating offline.

Cortex-A76 @ 2.4 GHz · 4GB LPDDR4X · 40-pin GPIO
Chemical / Vapor Sensing

E-Nose Gas Sensor Array

Combines Bosch BME688 (AI gas scanner), Sensirion SGP40 (digital VOC index), and MiCS-6814 (3-channel analog array) with a 16-bit ADS1115 ADC and active pneumatic micro-blower.

BME688 + SGP40 + MiCS-6814 + ADS1115 ADC
Physical / Dielectric Sensing

IWR6843AOPEVM mmWave Radar

Texas Instruments 60–64 GHz FMCW millimeter-wave radar with integrated Antenna-on-Package (AoP). Detects concealed dielectric bulk and structural anomalies without opening luggage.

60-64 GHz FMCW · 3 Tx / 4 Rx · 4GHz Bandwidth
Architectural Note: Why X-Ray is NOT Used in the Handheld System

Conventional X-ray inspection is intentionally not included in the handheld solution. Conventional X-ray machines are heavy, bulky, expensive, and generate ionizing radiation hazards requiring lead shielding and stationary gantries. Trinetra uses safe, non-ionizing 60–64 GHz mmWave radar + E-Nose gas sensing + Edge AI for rapid, lightweight first-line screening.

05 / DECISION SUPPORT LOGIC

Four-Case Screening Decision Matrix

Trinetra presents defensible operational classes rather than claiming absolute chemical identification. This provides actionable decision support for frontline RPF personnel.

Case 01
✓ NORMAL
Radar: Low · Gas: Low

Both physical structure and vapor emissions match ambient baseline conditions.

ACTION: Continue normal passenger clearance.
Case 02
⚠️ SUSPICIOUS
Radar: High · Gas: Low

Dense concealed object or shielded container detected without active vapor release.

ACTION: Secondary manual inspection under CCTV.
Case 03
⚠️ SUSPICIOUS
Radar: Low · Gas: High

Elevated VOC, solvent or precursor signature detected without structural anomaly.

ACTION: Inspect inner lining & swab baggage seams.
Case 04
🚨 HIGH RISK
Radar: High · Gas: High

Both dense physical anomaly and strong chemical vapor patterns confirmed.

ACTION: Immediate 50m cordon + Alert QRT / EOD.
